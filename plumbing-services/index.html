Start with what you can see

Most people don't know the trade's names for things. They know a sink won't drain, the shower runs cold, or there is a wet patch on the ceiling. Start there and match the symptom to a service.

Water going down slowly or not at all points to drain cleaning. If every drain in the house gurgles at once, the trouble is probably deeper in the main line, and a sewer line repair page may fit better. A toilet that keeps running or won't flush is covered under toilet repair.

Cold showers, a tank that rumbles, or water pooling at the base of the heater all belong with water heaters. Water heater repair fits a unit that can be saved. Water heater replacement fits one that is leaking from the tank or has run out of years.

Emergency or planned: how to decide

Some problems can wait for a weekday morning. A dripping faucet, a slow tub, or a new fixture you want installed are in this group. You can call, hear a clear price, and choose a day that suits you.

Others should not wait. Water spraying from a pipe, a sewage backup into a living area, a water heater leaking steadily, or no way to close the main valve all need help now. Call the 24/7 line, shut off what you can, and keep people away from wet electrical areas.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility first. That is never a call to make to a plumber before you are outside.

When you aren't sure which side of the line you are on, call and describe it. The dispatcher will tell you honestly whether it can wait. A burst pipe or an active leak is a call to place at once, while a faucet that drips once a minute usually can wait for the next day.

Finding a leak you can't see

A hissing sound in the wall, a water bill that has jumped, or a warm patch on the floor can mean a hidden leak. Leak detection uses equipment to locate the problem before anyone opens drywall or digs up a yard.

Outside, a soggy lawn with no rain or low pressure at every tap may point to the line from the street. That falls under water service line repair. Check your water utility's meter with every fixture off. If it keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere.

Bigger and planned jobs

Some work is a project, not a rescue. A tankless water heater changes how your home makes hot water. A repiping job replaces supply lines that keep failing. Plumbing installation covers new fixtures and layouts.

Older homes in the original plat were built from 1976 through the early 1980s, so they often have original supply lines and drains. If you own one, a planned look at the pipes costs far less than a flood. Businesses in the area can use our commercial plumbing service.

How pricing works across all services

We don't publish dollar amounts, because two jobs with the same name can differ a lot. Pipe depth, access, parts, the time of day and the condition of the old plumbing all move the price. The plumber assesses the job, then you hear a clear price before work starts.

If the cost is covered by insurance, ask your insurer. Sudden damage like a burst pipe is often covered, while slow leaks often are not, so read your policy.

Plumbing services questions

How do I know which service page matches my problem?

Look for the symptom you can see, such as a slow drain, a cold shower or a wet ceiling, and pick the page that names it. If two seem to fit, call the 24/7 line and describe it. The dispatcher will point you to the right job.

Do you handle both emergencies and planned work?

Yes. The line is open 24/7 for urgent problems, and the same line books planned work such as a fixture install or a water heater swap. Same-day appointments are offered when available.

Can you tell me the price before you come?

We can explain what drives the price on the call, but exact figures need a plumber to look at the job. You hear a clear price before work starts and can decline if it doesn't suit you.

What if my problem isn't listed?

Call anyway. Plumbing covers more than any list can show, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly if the job fits or if you need a different trade.

Not sure which service you need?

Call the 24/7 line, describe what is happening, and we will point you to the right job.
