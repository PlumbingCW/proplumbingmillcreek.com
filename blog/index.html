What you will find here

The posts are practical first. You will find step-by-step checks for common problems, plain explanations of what different repairs involve, and the factors that decide cost. We don't print dollar figures, because they vary too much from one house to the next.

Some jobs are fine for a careful homeowner, like clearing a simple hair clog or learning where your main shutoff is. Others are not. Anything involving gas, a 240 volt water heater circuit, a sewer line or a valve that won't close is marked as a stop-and-call job.

Local detail shows up where it helps. Many Mill Creek homes were built between 1976 and the early 1980s, mature trees shade many lots, and Western Washington is wet from fall through spring. Those facts shape which problems come up and how to prepare.

When reading is not enough

A guide can help you decide, but it can't see your pipes. If water is spreading, a drain is backing up into the house, or a heater is leaking, skip the reading and call the 24/7 line.
