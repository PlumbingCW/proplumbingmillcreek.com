Plumber in Thomas Lake, Mill Creek, WA

Thomas Lake is the northeast corner of Mill Creek, WA. The area joined the city in 2005, and it sits along 35th Avenue SE between 132nd Street SE and 144th Street SE. If you live here and need a plumber, call and give us your street address.

Because the area borders unincorporated Snohomish County, this page also covers a question that trips up many callers, which is whether your address is inside the City of Mill Creek at all.

What The Thomas Lake Area Is

In 2005 the Mill Creek City Council voted to annex the Northeast area, about 553 acres. News coverage at the time put it at about 900 homes and an estimated 2,200 to 2,300 residents. The annexation took effect on December 1, 2005, and a local paper called it the largest in the city's history.

  • SizeAbout 553 acres, annexed effective December 1, 2005.
  • Borders at annexation132nd Street SE to the north, Seattle Hill Road to the east, 144th Street SE to the south and the existing city limits to the west.
  • Main road35th Avenue SE runs through the area.
  • ShoppingThe Thomas Lake shopping center was part of the annexed area.
  • SubdivisionsThe city's housing division map lists Estates at Thomas Lake among its subdivisions.

Those figures come from 2005 news reports, so treat them as history and not as today's count. The county had also approved 262 planned homes in the area at that time, which means parts of it were still being built.

Is Your Thomas Lake Address Inside The City?

Seattle Hill Road forms part of the city's eastern boundary, so homes just across it may be in unincorporated Snohomish County. Mill Creek East, a census-designated place southeast of the city, also uses ZIP 98012 but is not part of the City of Mill Creek.

This matters because the permit office and the utility provider can differ by address. Ask the city and the county which office covers your street before you plan work that needs a permit. See our Mill Creek plumbing home page for the full list of services.

Plumbing Questions For A Growing Part Of Town

We do not have build dates for individual Thomas Lake streets, and an area that grew over time can differ a lot from one street to the next. In homes of any age, trouble tends to start in the same few places: the water heater, the supply lines, the shutoff valves and the drains.

A water heater at the end of its life is easy to overlook. Western Washington is earthquake country, so when you book water heater installation, ask whether the price includes strapping and any expansion tank the code requires.

The Water Line From The Meter To Your House

Often the water provider handles the meter and the main, and the homeowner handles the line from the meter to the house. Rules differ by provider, so ask yours. A soggy patch in the yard, a meter that keeps moving with everything off and a jump in your bill all point to a leak somewhere along that line.

Leak detection narrows down where the water is escaping before anyone digs. If the line itself has failed, our water service line repair page explains the options.

Water, Sewer And Permits

The City of Mill Creek lists two utility providers, Alderwood Water and Wastewater District and Silver Lake Water and Sewer District. Your bill shows which one serves your home. Washington's plumbing code, chapter 51-56 of the Washington Administrative Code, is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, and the building department can say whether your job needs a permit.

If You Find A Leak

  1. Shut off the water. Close the main valve where the line enters the house. If it will not turn, do not force it.
  2. Check the meter. With every fixture off, a moving meter dial means water is escaping somewhere.
  3. Protect the floor. Move rugs and electronics, and keep kids and pets out of the wet room.
  4. Call with details. Give the street address, the cross street and where the water is showing.

Thomas Lake Plumbing Questions

Is Thomas Lake part of the City of Mill Creek?

The Northeast annexation area, which the city annexed effective December 1, 2005, is. Addresses just across Seattle Hill Road may be in unincorporated Snohomish County, so check your exact address with the city or the county.

How many homes are in the Thomas Lake area?

News reports from 2005 said about 900 homes at the time of annexation, and the county had approved 262 more planned homes. The count today may differ, and the city can give a current figure.

Which office handles permits for plumbing work here?

It depends on whether your address is inside the City of Mill Creek or in unincorporated Snohomish County. Ask the city's building department first, and tell the plumber what they say.

Who is responsible for the water line between the meter and my house?

Often the homeowner is responsible for the line from the meter to the house, and the provider handles the meter and the main. Rules differ, so ask your provider before you spend money.

Can I call if I am not sure my address is inside the city?

Yes. Call, give us the street address and describe the problem. We will tell you whether we can help and what to expect before you decide.
