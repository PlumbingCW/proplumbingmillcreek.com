Drain Cleaning For Bothell Homes

Clogs are the most common reason people call us from this side of the county. Kitchen lines clog with grease, bath drains with hair, and main lines with roots and the buildup that comes with age. Our emergency drain cleaning service starts with a cable machine, and if the blockage keeps returning a camera shows what is actually in the pipe.

Many homes built from the 1980s onward have mature trees on the lot. Roots find small gaps in older sewer laterals, and one cleaning without a camera look may only buy a few weeks. If you have had two backups in a year, ask the plumber to scope the line before paying for another snaking.

Water Heater Repair And Replacement

A tank water heater from the 1990s or early 2000s is often on its second or third unit already. Signs it is nearing the end include rust-colored hot water, popping from the tank and moisture at the base. A water heater repair fixes a failed part such as an element or thermostat, but a leaking tank body is a replacement.

The honest rule is to repair when the tank is sound and the fault is cheap, and replace when the tank is old and leaking. Western Washington is earthquake country, so check that the heater is strapped to the wall. If it needs replacing, a plumber can set it up with proper straps in the same visit.

Finding Hidden Leaks

Not every leak announces itself. A meter that moves when everything is off, a warm patch on a slab floor or a musty smell in a closet can all mean a pipe is leaking somewhere you cannot see. Leak detection uses listening gear and moisture meters to narrow down the spot before anything is opened up.

Wet seasons make this worse. Rain from fall through spring keeps the ground saturated, and that can hide a slow underground leak or make a small one look like drainage. Checking your water utility's meter with every fixture off is a free first test.
